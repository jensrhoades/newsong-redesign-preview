## Why Design-Build Matters in a Kitchen Remodel

More renovation money gets wasted in kitchens than almost anywhere else, and most of it happens before construction starts. A homeowner hires a designer. The designer draws something beautiful. The homeowner gets attached. Then the contractor bids it and the number is $80,000 over what the homeowner had in mind. By then, walking back the design feels like losing something.

Design-build prevents that. When the contractor is in the room during design, before anything is drawn, before you've committed to a direction. Cost reality is part of every conversation. You know what a layout change costs before you decide to make it. You know what the cabinetry options cost before you fall in love with the custom option. The design that gets built is one you could actually afford before it was ever drawn.

One team, one point of contact. No gap between what was promised and what shows up.
