## Living in Grant Park, GA

Southeast of downtown Atlanta in the heritage rich expanse of Fulton County, Grant Park gathers century old homes around the city's oldest park. Queen Anne turrets peek above live oaks, and brick sidewalks lead toward lawns where families unroll blankets beside Zoo Atlanta's cheerful chorus. The neighborhood's grid catches light in a way that makes even quiet mornings feel cinematic.

Saturdays belong to the farmers market, where peaches perfume the air and musicians give the produce stalls a soundtrack. Oakland Cemetery rests nearby with marble angels and landscaped paths that read like a Victorian novel, equal parts history and horticulture. Restaurants serve Southern comfort with crisp edges, and corner coffee shops feel like living rooms for the street. Grant Park is generous with shade, generous with memory, and generous with the sense that home can be both elegant and easy.
