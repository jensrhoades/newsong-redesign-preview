## Frequently Asked Questions

**How is a home renovation different from a remodel?** While these terms are often used interchangeably, **home renovations** usually involve updating and improving existing spaces, whereas remodeling can include layout or structural changes. At NewSong Renovations, we do both, and often together.

**How long does a typical home renovation take?** Timelines vary based on scope and complexity. Smaller projects can take 2–3 months, while larger renovations may last 4–6 months or more. We provide clear timelines upfront.

**What does it cost to renovate a home in Atlanta?** Project costs depend on size, materials, and scope. We provide detailed proposals to help you plan accurately.

**Will I need permits?** Most major renovations do require permits. We handle all of the paperwork, applications, and coordination with local inspectors on your behalf.

**Can you work within a specific style or period design?** Absolutely. Whether you love clean modern lines or want to preserve historic details, we tailor our approach to suit your desired aesthetic.
