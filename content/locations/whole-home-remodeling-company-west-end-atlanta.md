## Living in West End Atlanta

Historic and deeply rooted in African-American heritage, West End in Atlanta, Fulton County, is a neighborhood rich with culture, activism, and architectural character. Known for its late 19th-century Queen Anne and Victorian homes, the area also includes craftsman bungalows and historic churches that tell the story of a resilient and proud community. The West End Historic District is listed on the National Register of Historic Places and serves as a cornerstone of cultural preservation.

In recent years, West End has welcomed renewed interest from artists, entrepreneurs, and families drawn to its sense of identity and growing number of businesses. The Atlanta BeltLine's Westside Trail cuts directly through the area, connecting residents to miles of walking and biking paths. Points of interest like the Wren's Nest, Hammonds House Museum, and a growing number of vegan restaurants and black-owned businesses underscore its cultural importance. West End is a neighborhood that proudly honors its past while embracing a future rooted in creativity and community.
