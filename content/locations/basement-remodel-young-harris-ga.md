A finished basement is one of the most valuable projects you can undertake in a mountain home, when it's done right. The problem is that in the mountains, "done right" requires a level of assessment that most basement finishing contractors don't start with.

Mountain terrain near Young Harris, Lake Chatuge, and Lake Nottely creates drainage and moisture conditions that flat-ground basements don't see. The grade affects how water moves around and under the foundation. Seasonal temperature swings affect moisture behavior inside the space. A basement finishing project that skips the site assessment and goes straight to framing and drywall is a project that will show you what got skipped, usually within a few years and usually in the most inconvenient way possible.
