## Living in Ormewood Park

Located on the east side of Atlanta in Fulton County, Ormewood Park is a leafy neighborhood full of Craftsman homes, shaded sidewalks, and a welcoming community spirit. Its development dates back to the early 1900s, and many homes reflect that period's architectural charm, though newer renovations and builds have brought fresh energy into the area. The neighborhood sits near Grant Park, Zoo Atlanta, and Glenwood Park, offering residents both green space and city life.

One of the defining features of Ormewood Park is its connection to the BeltLine's Southside Trail, which allows residents to bike or walk through some of Atlanta's most dynamic neighborhoods. Local shops, coffee houses, and small businesses reflect the neighborhood's strong support for local enterprise. The Ormewood Park Makers Festival and other community events help foster a tight-knit environment that balances creativity, family life, and a relaxed pace with direct access to the city's vibrant core.
