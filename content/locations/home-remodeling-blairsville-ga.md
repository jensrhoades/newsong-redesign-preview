## What Design-Build Means in Practice

The traditional renovation model has a built-in flaw: the contractor comes in after the design is finished. That means the cost-driving decisions, layout changes, structural work, scope of finishes, were all made without anyone in the room who knew what they cost. By the time the contractor bids the project, the homeowner is committed to a design that may be significantly over what they wanted to spend, and walking it back feels like losing something.

Design-build fixes this from the start. When the contractor is part of the design process, present before plans are drawn, before decisions are locked in. Cost reality is in every conversation. You know what a decision costs before you make it, not after you've already fallen in love with it. The scope holds because someone is accountable for holding it from day one.

One team, one conversation at every phase. That's how the design-build model actually works.
