## Living in Atlanta, GA

The city of Atlanta offers a unique blend of historical significance and contemporary innovation. Located primarily in Fulton County, it also stretches into DeKalb County and includes a rich mix of neighborhoods, each with its own character. From the bustling arts scene in Midtown to the historic sites tied to the civil rights movement, Atlanta presents an energetic atmosphere filled with diversity and pride.

Culture thrives in its vibrant food districts, music venues, and green spaces. The Atlanta BeltLine links parks, trails, and art installations, creating a shared space for both locals and visitors. Festivals, public murals, and major attractions like the Fox Theatre and Martin Luther King Jr. National Historical Park contributes to the city's layered identity.
