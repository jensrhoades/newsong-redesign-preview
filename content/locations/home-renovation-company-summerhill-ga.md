## Living in Summerhill, GA

Just south of downtown Atlanta in Fulton County, Summerhill is a neighborhood where history and modern revitalization meet. Once home to the city's early Jewish community and later an anchor for Black-owned businesses, the area carries layers of culture and resilience. Today, redeveloped storefronts and new housing bring a fresh energy while honoring the community's past.

The streets around Georgia State Stadium host local restaurants, breweries, and cafés that have quickly become gathering places. Public art and restored homes reflect the balance of heritage and progress, while neighborhood events foster a strong sense of connection among residents. Summerhill stands as an example of how Atlanta's neighborhoods reinvent themselves while holding onto their roots.
