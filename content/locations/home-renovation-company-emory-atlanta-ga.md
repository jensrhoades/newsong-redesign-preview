## Living in Emory, Atlanta, GA

Anchored by one of the South's most prestigious universities, the Emory neighborhood blends academic energy with the calm of a leafy, established residential community. Set just northeast of downtown in unincorporated DeKalb County, the area is defined by its mature tree canopy, rolling terrain, and a mix of historic homes and stately residences. Emory Village offers a walkable cluster of cafes and shops at the edge of campus, while the surrounding streets stay quiet and green. The presence of Emory University and the CDC gives the area a steady, professional character, drawing residents who value both culture and community.

Beyond the campus, Emory offers abundant access to green space, from the trails of Lullwater Preserve to the nearby Fernbank Forest and museum. The neighborhood sits within DeKalb County and shares its refined, preservation-minded character with adjacent Druid Hills. Residents enjoy the balance of a peaceful, walkable setting with quick access to the cultural and medical institutions that define this part of Atlanta. Well-kept historic homes, winding streets, and a strong sense of place give Emory a timeless appeal that has long made it one of the city's most desirable areas to call home.
