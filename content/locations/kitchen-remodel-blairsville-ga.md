## Why Design-Build Matters in a Kitchen

The design-build approach exists because the traditional model creates a predictable problem: the contractor comes in after the cost-driving decisions have already been made. Layout changes, cabinetry scope, structural modifications, all of it gets decided during design, without anyone in the room who knows what it costs. The bid that comes back surprises the homeowner because the homeowner was never in a real budget conversation during design.
