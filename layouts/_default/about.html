Who We Are

A design-build company you can trust.

As a design-build company, we work with you every step of the way to get the result you want. During the design process, we prioritize building relationships with our clients founded on trust and mutual understanding.

From there, we pride ourselves in maintaining transparent communication as our top-tier design and construction team diligently works to execute a project that is sure to exceed your expectations.

What Makes Us Different

Communication, trust, and accountability.

We prioritize maintaining great communication with our clients. We recognize that the presence of trust and accountability is central to any fruitful relationship.

We always aim to uphold our commitment to transparency and integrity, recognizing our clients as valued partners rather than mere transactions. Through open communication and genuine engagement, we continue to foster profound relationships with those we collaborate with.

Our Team

Meet the people behind NewSong.

Jason Smith

Owner

Jason has a deep-rooted passion for delivering exceptional client experiences through honest, transparent communication — honed over two decades. Beginning as a clean-up boy on his grandfather's residential projects, he absorbed the trade while fostering a love for craftsmanship. He holds a construction management degree from GA Southern University and founded NewSong after years in heavy industrial and residential construction. Outside work, you'll find Jason cooking, spending time with loved ones, and mastering putt putt.

Gabe Seibel

Pre-Construction Manager

With over a decade of experience spanning various construction roles, Gabe brings a wealth of experience to crafting exceptional spaces. His journey began as a handyman, evolved through managing high-end office facilities and building custom furniture and millwork, and culminated in five years as a renovation project manager. Beyond his day job, Gabe is quite the outdoorsman — he enjoys fly fishing and cycling, and even thru-hiked the entire Appalachian Trail.

David Gunter

Project Manager

David finds joy in bringing clients' dreams to life through meticulous planning, teamwork, and hands-on execution. From a childhood fascination with disassembling and reassembling toys to running a successful remodeling company for 15 years, David has always been drawn to piecing together tangible results from abstract ideas. Outside work, his greatest joy is time with his wife and four children — plus roasting coffee and a hidden dream of stand-up comedy.

Our Process

Built to keep you in control, from vision to completion.

Initial Consultation

We listen to your needs and learn exactly what your vision, preferences, and financial requirements are for your project.

Concept Phase

Our architects create 2–3 conceptual floor plans, capturing your ideas on paper and shaping an initial budget for your consideration.

Preconstruction

We turn concepts into full architectural plans, coordinate permitting and engineering, and build a detailed budget and proposal.

Construction

Our craftsmen bring your design to life with the best materials and constant communication — regular updates the whole way.

Guarantees

A finished product you'll be proud of.

We guarantee the best residential building experience you will ever have, along with a finished product that you'll be proud of for years to come — backed by a one-year written warranty on all workmanship and products.
